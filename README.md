
Understanding AI and LLM Basics
Introduction
This is my first project on Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Models (LLMs).
AI Tool Used
ChatGPT
Experiment 1 – Basic Prompt
Prompt:
What is Artificial Intelligence? Explain in simple words.
Output:
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that helps computers perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. It can learn, solve problems, and answer questions.
Experiment 2 – Improved Prompt
Prompt:
Explain Artificial Intelligence to a beginner in exactly 3 simple sentences with one real-life example.
Output:
Artificial Intelligence is a technology that allows computers to perform tasks that usually need human intelligence. It can understand questions, recognize patterns, and provide answers. For example, voice assistants use AI to understand and respond to our commands.
Comparison
The first prompt gave a basic explanation.
The improved prompt gave a clearer explanation with a real-life example.
Adding specific instructions improved the output.
What I Learned
AI helps computers perform intelligent tasks.
LLM stands for Large Language Model.
Prompts are instructions given to AI tools.
Clear and specific prompts can produce better answers.
Improving prompts helps us get more useful results.
Conclusion
This project helped me understand AI, LLM basics, and the importance of prompt engineering.
